A2A Kite Coordination Extension

Release candidate — not yet v1.0.0. The wire contract, the identifiers on this page and every signed preimage are fixed; the schemas, vectors and example agents are aligned to them. One release criterion is still open — the conformance suite's state-machine cases have no live driver yet (spec §9.1) — so the bundle is implementable but not frozen.

A versioned extension to the A2A protocol that lets buyer and seller agents form and execute agreements through the Kite Coordination Engine: typed, signed workflow commands; two-signature agreement over one termsHash; runtime-signed transition receipts; verifiable audit proof packages. No A2A fork, no Kite SDK required.

https://a2a.gokite.ai/extensions/coordination-workflow/v1
